# Publika Trading Co LLC

**Publika Building & Construction Materials Trading L.L.C**
ببليكا لتجارة مواد البناء ش.ذ.م.م

A privately held Dubai company supplying building and fit-out materials to architects, interior designers, developers, contractors, and private clients.

Dubai mainland licence **1094681**, issued **1 September 2022** and active until **31 August 2027**. The licence covers building-materials trading, sanitary-ware trading, warehouse operation, and wooden-products trading.

Warehouse 37, Span Precast, Dubai Investment Park 2, Dubai, UAE. P.O. Box 26344.

Public website: [publika.ae](https://publika.ae/)

## What we supply

- Sanitary ware, fittings, and commercial washroom systems
- Porcelain, mosaic, slab tiles, and engineered stone
- Wooden kitchens, wardrobes, vanities, and other wood products
- Specification support from design through supply
